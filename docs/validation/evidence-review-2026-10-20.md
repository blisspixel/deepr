## Boundaries

The experiments do not accept S0, qualify expertise, establish continuity's
value, promote embedding routing by default, widen provider execution or
write canonical knowledge. Tiny authored worlds and fixed brief synthesis
cannot measure real-domain research or maintenance quality. A missing known
condition remains a missing condition even when a model safely reports a
context gap. Source membership, hashes and quotation matches are identity and
transport facts; semantic review supplies the bounded judgments.
