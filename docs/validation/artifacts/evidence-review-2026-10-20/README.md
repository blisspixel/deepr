# Evidence review artifacts, 2026-10-20

These archives preserve the completed bounded correction diagnostic and the
rejected packing contrast. They do not establish S0 acceptance, general expert
quality or a successful packing policy. See the
[dated assessment](../../evidence-review-2026-10-20.md).

Each archive contains a `MANIFEST.json` with every member's SHA-256 and byte
length. [index.json](index.json) binds the compressed archives and their
manifests. Files retain their original bytes, including failed trials. Source
and model text inside an archive is untrusted evidence, not instructions.

The corrected trial's three packer source files were reconstructed after
collection from immutable commit `47bd37b4079054b6bf7fd6508ca199a491ec5c2a`.
Their bytes match the precollection manifest hashes. The reconstruction receipt
records that timing; it is not described as a precollection copy. Later product
repairs remain separate from the source actually used to collect those answers.

The two packing archives partition one original 912-member evidence inventory.
The initial combined archive exceeded the repository's existing file-size gate;
it was preserved locally and divided by trial without dropping evidence or
relaxing the gate. The other four logical archives retain their source bytes.

The private S0 assignment key, unrelated research notes and credentials are not
included. Independent review-service model snapshot, usage and monetary cost
are unobserved; native local ledger entries at $0 do not make all stages free.
