# Expert profile: show understanding and its evidence

Status: implementation candidate, 2026-09-20.

The profile currently opens a claims table. Its numeric confidence estimates
combine source limits and age decay, but the page gives readers little basis
for interpreting them. Repetitive claims about repository commands also say
little about the Python expert's ability to explain or advise.

The first repair uses the existing retained-source study and briefing loop.
Build a substantive example, preserve its inputs, and examine consultations
against questions excluded from construction. Document failures and coverage
limits. Presentation and source counts cannot certify expertise. No confidence
floor, score inflation, generated example answers, or hand-written canonical
brief may substitute for that work.

The profile will open a Perspective view over the existing read-only `hold`,
`noticed`, and `corpus` routes. It shows orientation, positions, reasoning,
supporting findings, conditions for revision, unresolved questions, and sources.
Claim inspection remains directly addressable. Opening a profile performs no
research, synthesis, learning, or paid work.

Missing briefing and failed retrieval are distinct states. An absent briefing
asks the operator to study and brief the expert; it does not manufacture an
orientation from its name. A failed request offers a retry and does not become
an empty research record. Source links accept only HTTP(S). Dates describe
recorded study or acquisition, never an inferred substantive review or a check
of the caller's current environment.

This increment does not implement default consultation preparation. The
[living-expertise delivery plan](../plans/living-expertise.md) owns that work.
The profile explicitly distinguishes stored research from a current check.
Existing claim estimates remain unchanged and inspectable with an explanation;
no overall probability of expert correctness is displayed.

The human-readable consultation heading likewise shows the expert name without
an unexplained packet-confidence grade. Existing JSON fields stay compatible;
the stored context and its evidence remain available for inspection.

A finding can have both matching and unmatched evidence excerpts. Preserve
that distinction in the evidence disclosure; its finding-level grounding flag
does not certify every excerpt or establish the interpretation's correctness.

Verify default and explicit navigation, populated and absent perspectives,
partial retrieval failure, unsafe links, long text, and narrow screens. Inspect
the rendered profile against real retained state. A README capture must have a
dated validation record and must not imply broader qualification than the
consultations actually examined.
