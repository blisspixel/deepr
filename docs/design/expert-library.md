# A library of persistent domain experts

Status: direction updated 2026-10-29. Replaces the June 20 always-current fleet
and automatic capacity-waterfall framing. Historical runs do not qualify
current advice, adapters, or costs. See [Supported Surface](../SUPPORTED_SURFACE.md).

## Value

A library lets a human or host find a continuing specialist with retained
research, explanations, perspective, experience, and methods. The same expert
can investigate a question and later explain what changed. Its knowledge and
identity can be portable without sharing credentials or unrestricted writes.
[What an expert is](what-an-expert-is.md) defines the product.

One expert must first demonstrate useful supervised work and learning. A roster
can contain many well-formed but unqualified experts; quantity, timestamps,
claim-support estimates, and maintenance activity do not prove expertise.

## Capabilities and direction

Local formation, explicit maintenance, stored-context consultation, derived
knowledge views, and bounded local investigations provide the substrate.
Experimental prepared consultation is scoped, not generally default-on.
All production plan adapters currently fail execution gates. Local inference
has zero external model-provider cost only under its owned endpoint proof;
hardware, energy, and operator time are still resources. There is no automatic
metered fallback.

The next connection is the [persistent expert session](persistent-expert-agent.md):
inspect memory, investigate, observe, propose revision, review, and reuse.
Library-wide maintenance and larger teams follow demonstrated need and existing
S4/S5 gates. They are not prerequisites for that session.

## Teams and routing

Candidate routing can use domain metadata, text, embeddings, and graph links.
Scores select candidates; they do not establish that an expert knows the answer.
The selected expert must inspect evidence and disclose its limits.

A bounded team is useful when independent questions benefit from separate
contexts. Each participant returns evidence, interpretations, gaps, and
uncertainty. Preserve dissent and source ancestry; model agreement is not
independent verification. The owner controls shared memory admission, and
children share parent resource and cancellation bounds.

General organization, cross-project task allocation, and external actions
remain host responsibilities. The [harness boundary](agentic-harness-boundary.md)
allows resumable domain episodes without making Deepr a company orchestrator.
Paperclip, Pi, Hermes, and Grok inform different parts of that composition;
see the [research review](../research/expert-agent-systems-2026-10-29.md).
